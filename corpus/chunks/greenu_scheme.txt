About The Scheme
GreenU is a campus-wide reward scheme aiming at promoting HKU members to act sustainably.HKU members can earn GreenU Points from their sustainability actions and redeem rewards.
How to Earn GreenU Points?
HKU students and staff can access the GreenU function in the HKU App (with valid HKU portal accounts) to collect GreenU Points from their sustainability actions.
Currently, GreenU Points can be earned from the following sustainability actions:
Recycling using HKU Smart Recycling Bins
Participating in green campaigns/events/activities under the GreenU Reward Scheme
More sustainability actions will be put under the Scheme to give more encouragement to HKU members to act green and earn GreenU Points.
Who can join this reward scheme?
Any HKU student or staff having a valid HKU portal account can join the reward scheme
Will the GreenU Points expire?
All the GreenU Points will be valid for gift redemption as long as your HKU portal account is active.
Your GreenU Points will be forfeited when your HKU portal account is expired because of graduation or termination of employment contract.
