The Correct Choices
Paper
DOs
Envelopes · Letter paper · Paper folders
Office paper
Corrugated paper · Toilet paper roll core
Standard paper business cards
Non-adhesive packaging paper
Brochures · Publications · Books · Telephone books · Non-confidential reports
DON'Ts
Adhesive parts of the sticky notes
Tin wrapping paper · Auto-adhesive · Glued-stickers paper
Film copy paper for projectors
Blue drawings · Cellophane
Napkins · Toilet paper
Paper tableware
Adhesive paper · Wax paper
Thermal induction fax paper
Paper and cardboard contaminated with paint, chemicals, food, etc.
Metals
DOs
Aluminium cans and iron cans ONLY (soda cans, Coke cans, beverage cans, tin cans, drink cans)
DON'Ts
Aerosol cans
Potato chips cans
Paint cans
Beer bottle caps
Cookware
Chemical containers
Plastics
DOs
Beverage plastic bottles (water bottles, Coke bottles, soda bottles, plastic drink bottles)
Plastic containers
Plastics bottles for personal care products (shampoo bottles, body wash bottles, soap bottles, lotion bottles)
Plastic utensils
Packaging materials
CD and Polyfoam | Styrofoam
1 PET
2 HDPE
3 PVC
4 LDPE
5 PP
6 PS
DON'Ts
Medicine containers
Correction fluid container
Toothpaste tube
Plastic products with metals
Rubber and silicon products
Other composite materials
Glass Bottles
DOs
Beverage, food and sauce glass bottles ONLY (wine bottles, beer bottles, juice glass bottles, sauce bottles)
DON'Ts
Glass containers or bottles contaminated with hazardous chemicals
Tempered · Laminated · Strengthened glass
Rechargeable Batteries
DOs
All types of portable rechargeable batteries generated from households
DON'Ts
Single use batteries
Lead acid batteries
Damaged batteries without protection measures
Tetra Pak
DOs
All types of Tetra Pak Cartons (milk cartons, juice boxes, beverage cartons, drink boxes)
DON'Ts
With straws and plastic wrappings without a corner cut
