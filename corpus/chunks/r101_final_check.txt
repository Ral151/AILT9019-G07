Final Check Before Recycling
Contaminants can cause entire truckloads of recyclables to end up as garbage, it does take a little bit of work to prepare items for recycling, but the effort is well worth the environmental benefits.
Here are some guidelines to ensure each of your recyclable deposits are properly recycled.
General Guidelines
They should be dry and clean.
They should be able to fit inside the recycling cabinets.
They should be placed in the cabinet designated for its recyclable group.
For metals, plastics, glass bottles
Bottles, containers and cans should be rinsed and dried before recycling.
You should remove the caps and sleeves from the bottles and containers.
For paper
You should remove all non-paper materials such as plastic coated pages and plastic tape before recycling.
For Tetra Pak
You should remove straws and plastic wrappings, then cut a corner and flatten them.
They should be rinsed and dried before recycling.
Rechargeable Batteries
The battery terminal of the rechargeable battery should be covered with masking tape.
As batteries are vulnerable to damages, please place them in a plastic bag and seal the bag with adhesive tape before recycling. You may also reuse the packaging of the new batteries to pack the recycling batteries.
