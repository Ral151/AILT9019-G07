Smart Recycling Bins
With the GreenU function in the HKU app, HKU students and staff can use the HKU Smart Recycling Bins for recycling and gain GreenU Points.
Smart Recycling Bins by HKU accept the following recyclables
Paper (office paper, cardboard, brochures)
Metal (aluminium cans, iron cans, soda cans)
Plastic bottle (water bottles, Coke bottles, soda bottles, except at Central Podium Levels and Pine Court)
Other plastic (plastic containers, packaging materials)
Glass bottle (wine bottles, beer bottles, sauce bottles, only at Central Podium Levels and Pine Court)
