Disposable Plastic Free Campus Policy
Source: The University of Hong Kong (https://www.hku.hk/en/about-hku/policies/disposable-plastic-free-campus-policy)

HKU implemented its Disposable Plastic Free Campus Policy in September 2020.

The policy bans the following disposable plastic items on campus:
- Plastic bags
- Plastic bottles of 1 litre or less in volume
- Plastic cutlery (including forks, knives, and spoons)

Plastic items used in health-related or other emergency situations are explicitly allowed as exceptions.

The policy aims to reduce the consumption of single-use disposable plastics on campus and encourage the use of reusable alternatives.

Campus measures that support Bring Your Own (B.Y.O.):
- Takeaway Discounts at some catering outlets when using your own containers
- A reusable tableware rental service at Bijas Vegetarian Restaurant
- A "Borrow A Cup" system at Starbucks in the Composite Building

From April 2024, the Hong Kong-wide ban on disposable plastic tableware for takeaway customers also applies. The banned items for takeaway include:
- Expanded polystyrene (EPS/foam) tableware (including cups, bowls, and food boxes)
- Plastic forks, knives, and spoons
- Plastic straws
Plastic cups, bowls, and food boxes are banned for dine-in services only.

Recommended reusable choices include reusable plastic bottles, containers made from coffee grounds, and stainless-steel insulated bottles. Suitable materials for reusable cutlery include stainless steel, wood, bamboo, and silicone, which are durable and washable.
