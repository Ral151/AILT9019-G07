Recycling 101 - The Correct Choice
Source: HKU Waste Reduction (https://www.wastereduction.hku.hk/recycling-101)

Here is a complete guide to master recycling at the re Kiosk and Smart Recycling Bins. The recyclable categories are Paper, Tetra Pak, Metal, Glass Bottle, Plastic, and Rechargeable Battery.

Paper Recycling
Accepted paper items include newspapers, flyers, magazines, textbooks, printed A4 paper, carton boxes, cardboard boxes, and office paper. Paper must be clean and dry. Greasy or food-contaminated paper cannot be recycled and should go to general refuse. Remove any non-paper attachments such as plastic windows, staples, and tape before recycling. Flatten cardboard boxes to save space.

Tetra Pak Recycling
Tetra Pak and beverage cartons (milk cartons, juice boxes) are accepted at the re Kiosk. Rinse the carton before recycling. Flatten it to save space. Tetra Pak is a separate category from paper and plastic because it is a multi-layered composite material.

Metal Recycling
Accepted metal items include aluminium soft-drink cans, tin cans, and metal food cans. Rinse the cans before recycling. Remove lids where possible. Aerosol cans are not accepted in general recycling bins because they may be pressurized.

Glass Bottle Recycling
Glass bottles are accepted at the re Kiosk and at the Smart Recycling Bins located at G/F, Central Podium Levels (Centennial Campus) and Pine Court. Only clean glass bottles are recycled. Other glass items such as glass mirrors, drinking glasses, fluorescent tubes, and light bulbs cannot be placed in the glass recycling bin and require special handling. Rinse glass bottles before recycling. Do not break the glass.

Plastic Recycling
Accepted plastic items include PET plastic drink bottles, clean plastic packaging, plastic bags, and plastic takeaway containers (if rinsed clean). Plastic bottles are accepted at most Smart Recycling Bins except at Central Podium Levels and Pine Court, which accept other plastics instead. Plastic must be clean and dry. Greasy or food-contaminated plastic cannot be recycled and should go to general refuse. Rinse plastic containers and remove food residue before recycling. Single-use plastic cutlery may be accepted as other plastic at some locations; follow on-site signage.

Cups and Beverage Containers
Milk tea cups and other beverage cups should be rinsed to remove liquid and residue before sorting. If the cup is made of plastic, it goes into the plastic recycling bin after rinsing. Paper cups with a plastic coating (plastic-coated paper cups) are usually not recyclable because of the plastic lining and should follow official guidance or on-site signage. Empty and clean aluminium cans go into the metal recycling bin.

Rechargeable Battery Recycling
Rechargeable batteries are accepted at the re Kiosk only. Rechargeable batteries are hazardous waste and must not be placed in general recycling bins or general refuse. The re Kiosk is the designated collection point for rechargeable batteries on campus. For other types of batteries or hazardous waste, consult the official guidance and follow the designated collection procedures.

Final Check Before Recycling
- Make sure the item is clean and dry.
- Remove food residue, grease, and liquids.
- Flatten items to save space.
- Remove non-recyclable attachments (lids, caps, plastic windows, tape).
- When in doubt, check the on-site signage or ask the cleaner.
- Contaminated items go to general refuse, not the recycling bins.
