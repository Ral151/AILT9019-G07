GreenU Reward Scheme
Source: HKU Waste Reduction (https://www.wastereduction.hku.hk/greenu-reward-scheme)

About The Scheme
GreenU is a campus-wide reward scheme aiming at promoting HKU members to act sustainably. HKU members can earn GreenU Points from their sustainability actions and redeem rewards. Act green to earn more.

How to Earn GreenU Points?
HKU students and staff can access the GreenU function in the HKU App (with valid HKU portal accounts) to collect GreenU Points from their sustainability actions. Currently, GreenU Points can be earned from the following sustainability actions:
- Recycling using HKU Smart Recycling Bins
- Participating in green campaigns/events/activities under the GreenU Reward Scheme
More sustainability actions will be put under the Scheme to give more encouragement to HKU members to act green and earn GreenU Points.

GreenU Point Conversion Rate for Recycling:
- Paper: 10 points per kg
- Plastic bottles: 80 points per kg
- Other plastics, metal, glass: points vary; see the GreenU function in the HKU App for the latest conversion rate.
- A minimum of 0.1 kg is required per transaction to register and earn points.
- During promotional periods, double points may apply.

Rewards
Rewards available for redemption include: Carbon Wallet Points Coupon, Silicon Food Container, Silicone Foldable Water Bottle, Double Sided Tote Bag, Bamboo Stylus Pen, Bubble Tea Tumbler, Bubble Tea Thermal Tumbler, HKU Bear with Hoodie, HKU Bear Keychain series, Household Cleaning Tablets, and Natural Loofah Washing Sheet. Check the GreenU function in the HKU App for availability and redeem with your GreenU Points. Terms and conditions apply.

FAQs
Why GreenU? GreenU is a campus-wide reward scheme for HKU students and staff. HKU students and staff can easily access the GreenU function through the HKU App to earn GreenU Points and redeem gifts, without installing an additional app. Gifts redemption can be conducted on campus and/or online.

Who can join this reward scheme? Any HKU student or staff having a valid HKU portal account can join the reward scheme.

Can I also earn other platforms' rewards in the GreenU Reward Scheme? Currently, the earned GreenU Points can only redeem gifts provided by HKU. Collaboration with other reward platforms is being explored.

Will the GreenU Points expire? All the GreenU Points will be valid for gift redemption as long as your HKU portal account is active. Your GreenU Points will be forfeited when your HKU portal account is expired because of graduation or termination of employment contract.

How to use Smart Recycling Bins to earn GreenU Points:
Step 1: Press the button to start the session.
Step 2: Scan your HKU Access Code from the HKU App.
Step 3: Start recycling your materials.
The smart bin automatically records the weight of deposited recyclables and credits GreenU Points to your account.
