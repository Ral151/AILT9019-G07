re Kiosk

The re Kiosk is just the beginning of a series of recycling initiatives. With the support and participation of the university community, the re Campaign will be empowered to expand its network and scale on campus.

Location of re Kiosk: Run Run Shaw Podium, Main Campus (next to Subway and opposite to the Runme Shaw Building). The re Kiosk offers 24-hour access.

re Kiosk accepts these recyclables: Paper, Tetra Pak, Metal, Glass Bottle, Plastic, and Rechargeable Battery.

Green Features of re Kiosk: The re Kiosk is designed and constructed in a sustainable manner. A wide variety of plants are grown there, turning the spot into a mini garden with beautiful greenery. With sustainability at the heart of the design, it reinforces HKU's institutional vision, commitments, and goals into all aspects of the University's facilities, operations and activities.

Why re Kiosk?
- Recycle more: More types of recyclables will be collected at the new kiosk, such as rechargeable batteries, and different types of plastics other than just bottles and beverage cartons.
- Easy quantification: The recyclables collected by the kiosk can be evaluated easily so our campus is well prepared for the implementation of the Municipal Solid Waste Charging.
- As an icon: The re Kiosk will become the visual icon of HKU's devotion to be a pioneer in promoting green living.
- Facilitate waste reduction and recycling: This large-scale, well-managed and user-friendly recycling site can facilitate waste reduction and recycling support for members of HKU, meaning everyone can contribute easily.
- Education: The iconic, all-rounded kiosk is more educative than the scattered recycling bins.

Smart Recycling Bins

With the GreenU function in the HKU app, HKU students and staff can use the HKU Smart Recycling Bins for recycling and gain GreenU Points.

How to recycle at HKU Smart Recycling Bins with the GreenU function:
Step 1: Press the button to start the session.
Step 2: Scan your HKU Access Code from the HKU App.
Step 3: Start recycling your materials.

Smart Recycling Bins by HKU accept the following recyclables: Paper, Metal, Plastic bottle (except at Central Podium Levels and Pine Court), Other plastic, and Glass bottle (only at Central Podium Levels and Pine Court).

Smart Recycling Bins network on campus:

Main Campus:
- LG2/F, K.K. Leung Building (outside lift lobby)
- Meng Wah Complex (outside 1/F entrance, next to the escalator)
- G/F, Hui Oi Chow Science Building (outside Innovation Wing)
- G/F, Central Podium Levels, Centennial Campus

Sassoon Road Campus:
- G/F, Jockey Club Building for Interdisciplinary Research (outside the entrance)
- Laboratory Block, Faculty of Medicine Building (G/F lift lobby, near food kiosk)

Senior Staff Quarters:
- Pine Court (Common Area, outside Block 1)

Smart Recycling Bins accept these recyclables: Paper, Metal, Plastic, and Glass Bottle.

Features of the Smart Recycling Bins:
- 24-hour operation
- Self-serving recycling
- With sensors monitoring fill levels for efficient recyclable collection
- Automatic recording of the weight of recyclables
- Earn GreenU points for reward redemption
