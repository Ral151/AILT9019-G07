transcribed text below, and confirm every English word, punctuation mark and
capitalisation matches the image. Tick `[x]` when correct; mark any discrepancy
at the end of the line with `←` and the actual wording.

After reviewing, set `review.status` to `reviewed` for that source in
`corpus/manifest.yaml` (and fill in `reviewer` and `reviewed_at`).

